# UPSHIFT product scope

## Core product

- AI tutor and career coach answer roadmap and career questions.
- Daily learning tasks, assessments, skill-gap analysis, streaks, and role-focused roadmaps help students build job readiness.
- Mock interviews provide answer-level scores and feedback. Camera and screen previews remain optional local controls; the app does not claim to measure eye contact or confidence.
- Skill demand signals come from active job postings saved on the platform. They are shown with the number of matching postings so students can judge the evidence.
- English, Hindi, and Hinglish are supported in AI tutor/coach replies. Mock interview questions and speech transcription can use English or Hindi/Hinglish.

## Phase 2 / future scope

AR/VR is an exploratory extension, not a core dependency or a currently shipped capability. The first realistic experiment would be a browser-based VR mock-interview room connected to the existing interview practice flow. Keep any eye-contact, pace, or confidence measurements opt-in, explain their limits, and validate accessibility before treating them as hiring signals.

Other possible experiments:

- Virtual labs for networking or electronics practice.
- AR visualizations for data structures and operating-system concepts.
- Virtual workplace tours and group-discussion practice.

## Good-to-have backlog

- Verified proof-of-work through GitHub or project review.
- Low-bandwidth mode and further mobile performance work.
